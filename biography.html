An inspiring football journey


Cristiano Ronaldo is a very famous football player. Born into a poor family, Cristiano started taking part in football at a really young age and was chosen to the international team at the age of 18. In an exceedingly very short time, Cristiano managed to form an area within the hearts of the people through his game and currently he has become the foremost famous player in the world.

Early life and Family


Cristiano Ronaldo dos Santos Aveiro was born on February 5, 1985, in Funchal, Madeira, Portugal. His father named him "Ronaldo" after Ronald Reagan, an American actor who later became the President of the United States. Cristiano Ronaldo is the captain of the Portugal national football team. Today, he earns enough to live in a luxurious mansion in Saudi Arabia; however, you may be surprised to know that his life has not always been like this. As a child, Ronaldo lived with his family in a small, tin-roofed house. His family consisted of his parents, one older brother, and two older sisters. Born into a poor working-class family, his father worked as a municipal gardener and a part-time kit man for a local club. Cristiano Ronaldo started playing football at an early age. He loved the sport since childhood. Seeing his passion for the game, he joined the Andorinha Sports Club at the age of eight. As a child, Ronaldo would often cry whenever his team lost or he didn't play well. His focus was much more on the football pitch than at school. Because of his talent, at the age of 10, he was signed by Nacional, a major local sports club. After observing his outstanding talent, Sporting CP, a major club located in Lisbon (the capital of Portugal), signed him for a fee of £1,500. Because of this, Ronaldo had to leave his home in Madeira and move to Lisbon to pursue his football career.
